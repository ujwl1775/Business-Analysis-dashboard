Amazon India Dashboard
Project Overview
The Amazon India Dashboard is designed as a simple and easy-to-understand management dashboard for Manoj to submit to his manager, Ravi, who comes from a non-technical background. The dashboard brings key business information together in one place using simple charts, graphs, and KPIs.
Business Objective
The dashboard provides a clear overview of Sales, Profit, Products, Orders, Customer Order Status, Payment, Fulfilment, and Geographic Performance. It is intended to help management quickly understand overall business performance without requiring technical knowledge.
Key Dashboard Areas
1. Overall Sales & Profit Performance
Business Question: How is the business performing overall?
Dashboard should show:
•	Total Sales
•	Total Profit
•	Total Orders
•	Total Units Sold
•	Average Order Value
•	Profit Margin %
•	Monthly Sales & Profit Trend
Expected Insight: Manoj can quickly understand whether sales and profitability are improving or declining over time.
2. Category & Product Performance
Business Question: Which products and categories are driving the business?
Dashboard should show:
•	Category-wise Sales
•	Category-wise Profit
•	Category-wise Units Sold
•	Top 10 Products by Sales
Expected Insight: Manoj can identify high-performing categories/products and products that may need attention.
3. Order Status & Revenue Loss
Business Question: How many orders are successfully completed and how many are being lost?
Dashboard should show:
•	Delivered Orders
•	Shipped Orders
•	Returned Orders
•	Cancelled Orders
•	Return Rate %
•	Cancellation Rate %
•	Category-wise Returns & Cancellations
Expected Insight: Manoj can understand the impact of returns and cancellations on overall business performance.
4. Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Performance
Business Question: Which payment methods, fulfillment methods and states are generating business?
Dashboard should show:
•	Sales by Payment Method
•	Profit by Payment Method
•	Sales by Fulfillment Method
•	Profit by Fulfillment Method
•	State-wise Sales
•	State-wise Profit
•	State-wise Orders
Expected Insight: Manoj can identify important markets and understand how different payment and fulfillment channels contribute to business performance.
README Summary
This project presents an Amazon India management dashboard focused on sales, profitability, products, orders, customer order status, payment methods, fulfillment methods, and geographic performance. The dashboard is structured to make business information easy to understand through KPIs, charts, graphs, and trends, enabling management to monitor performance and identify areas that require attention.
